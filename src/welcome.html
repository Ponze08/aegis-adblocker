You’re protected.

Aegis is already blocking ads, trackers and dangerous sites on every website. There is nothing to set up, but here are three things worth knowing.

1

Pin Aegis to your toolbar

Click the puzzle piece at the top right of the browser, then the pin next to Aegis. One click on the shield shows what was blocked on the page, and lets you turn protection off for a site.

2

Pick your extras

Optional filters for a calmer web. Turn them on or off anytime from Filter lists.

3

If a site misbehaves

Once in a while, blocking can break a feature. Open the popup, then switch the site to Basic or turn protection off for that site only. Aegis remembers your choice for each site.
